# Rental Item Reporting

## Epic Information
**Epic Name:** Rental Item Reporting

**Linked Product Module:** Rental Management for Business Central (Fixed Asset integration)

**Linked Product Vision Functional Requirements:**
- FR-2: Rental pricing, invoicing plans, and returns (revenue source for the report)

## Overview
**Summary:** Provide a rental item history report that shows, for each rental item, its rental history with a focus on rental revenue and the related cost.

**Problem Statement:** Finance and operations users cannot see, in one place, how much revenue each rental item has earned and what cost is associated with it. Rental activity and item cost are spread across rental records and the fixed asset record, which makes profitability and utilisation per item slow to assess.

**MoSCoW Priority:** Must have

## Strategic Context
**ICPs Impacted:**
- ICP A: Equipment rental operators running Business Central

**Key Personas Supported:**
- Finance / AR user
- Rental manager or operations lead

**Business Value:** Gives per-item rental revenue and related cost in one report, supporting profitability analysis, pricing decisions, and fleet utilisation reviews.

## Success Criteria
- Users can open a report for a single rental item and see its rental history for a selected period.
- The report shows rental revenue and related cost for each rental transaction and in total.
- Totals reconcile to posted rental invoices for the same item and period.

## Scope
### In Scope
- Rental item history report filtered by item and date range
- Rental revenue per rental transaction and total
- Related cost per rental transaction and total
- Link to the rental item's fixed asset record (per the Fixed Asset integration)

### Out of Scope
- Changes to rental pricing, invoicing, or posting logic
- Changes to standard fixed asset depreciation or book value
- Cost allocation methods beyond those already recorded on rental transactions (TBD)
